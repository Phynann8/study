About Khmer Code 🇰🇭

Empowering the next generation of Cambodian developers through localized, world-class education.

Our Mission

At Khmer Code, we believe that language and cost should never be barriers to learning technology. We provide high-quality C# and Web development training using metaphors from daily Cambodian life.

The Vision

We aim to create a vibrant tech ecosystem where students can learn for free, build their portfolios in our marketplace, and get hired by top local companies.
